Research

Applied AI for Construction Intelligence

My research applies vision-language models and video AI to safety detection, progress tracking, and decision support on construction sites.

Statement

Construction sites produce a lot of visual information, but little of it is captured in a usable form. Workers, machines, materials, and hazards interact in highly dynamic ways - yet most site management still relies on manual observation, paper-based reporting, and subjective judgment.

My PhD research at Purdue University asks how AI models can reliably interpret what happens on a construction site.

I work specifically on Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and video foundation models - systems that can jointly process visual inputs (images, video streams) and generate semantic understanding in natural language. The applications range from automated PPE detection and unsafe behavior flagging, to temporal progress tracking across construction phases, to intelligent documentation generation.

My background in civil/geotechnical engineering, combined with graduate training in big data analytics and deep learning, helps me connect the technical side of AI with the practical constraints of the construction industry.

Two newer threads run through my work in 2026. The first is trustworthy, explainable AI: with Prof. Mustafa Abdallah, I built ConstructionSafety-FaithBench to test whether safety VLMs actually rely on the visual evidence behind their verdicts (SNAS 2026, oral). The second is egocentric perception: the Project Aria Gen 2 research proposal I developed and submitted was accepted, and the lab was awarded five pairs of Meta's research glasses for studying construction work from the worker's own point of view.

Focus Areas
01

Vision-Language Models in Construction

VLMs like GPT-4V, LLaVA, and emerging open-source models can process construction site images and video frames while answering natural language queries about what they observe. My work investigates how to fine-tune, prompt, and deploy these models for:

  • Real-time PPE and safety compliance detection
  • Worker activity recognition and task classification
  • Hazard identification and automatic reporting
  • Equipment and material status monitoring

02

Video Intelligence & Progress Tracking

Construction projects unfold over time - video models that understand spatiotemporal dynamics can automate the tedious task of progress documentation, comparing real-time site footage against BIM models and project schedules.

03

Generative AI for AEC Workflows

LLMs and multimodal generative models can help automate construction documentation, RFI responses, and decision support - reducing administrative overhead while improving accuracy and traceability.
