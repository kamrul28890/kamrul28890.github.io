Research awardFall 2026

Our Project Aria Gen 2 research proposal was accepted

Meta's Project Aria program accepted the research proposal I developed and submitted, and awarded five Aria Gen 2 research glasses to a research lab at Purdue. The devices capture egocentric video, eye gaze, spatial audio and motion for studying construction work from the worker's point of view.

ConstructionSafety-FaithBench at SNAS 2026

My paper on explainability for construction-safety vision-language models was accepted as an oral presentation at the 5th SNAS Interdisciplinary Research Conference at George Mason University. I presented it in October, and the proceedings are forthcoming. The work also received an SNAS Travel Grant for Doctoral Students.

About

Engineer. Researcher.
Curious.

I started as a civil engineer in Bangladesh, built foundations in geotechnics, pivoted through finance, returned to data science, and am now a PhD student at Purdue, building AI tools for practical construction problems.

My research lives at the intersection of Vision-Language Models (VLMs), video understanding, and construction management, using AI to automatically detect safety hazards, track project progress, and support everyday decisions on site.
